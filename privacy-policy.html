Website / Privacy

Privacy policy.

Draft updated November 16, 2026 — review before publication.

This draft describes the proposed V2 website. Hosting, advertising and service settings should be verified before publishing.

Contact information

Formspree processes the name, email and message you submit. Email enquiries are handled by the relevant mail provider.

Technical information

Hosting and third-party services may process IP addresses, browser details and requested URLs for delivery and security. This redesign does not enable private visitor-by-visitor IP logging.

Local storage and third parties

The website saves cookies_accepted=true to remember when you dismiss the storage notice. It is not an advertising consent system. Pressing Accept does not change when third-party scripts run. The homepage uses Google reCAPTCHA; forms use Formspree.

Advertising and security

The V2 pages do not add Google AdSense scripts, but existing advertising settings require a separate review. Any future visitor-IP monitoring will need clear notice, restricted access and a documented retention period.

Your choices

You can clear stored preferences using your browser's site-data settings. Contact-form retention depends on Formspree account settings. For privacy questions or deletion requests email contact@nawazsharif.in.
