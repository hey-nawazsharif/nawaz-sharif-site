Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

A clear account of what this site collects, which services are involved, how information may be used and what choices you have. This policy describes the current personal website; separate commercial services will require additional notices where applicable.

1. Who operates this website

nawazsharif.in is operated as the personal and professional website of Nawaz Sharif. This notice explains how information may be handled when you visit the site, read content or send an enquiry. Privacy-related requests can be sent to contact@nawazsharif.in. A separate incorporated business entity is not represented by this notice.

2. Information that may be collected

  • Information you submit: name, email address, message content and any context voluntarily included in an enquiry.
  • Technical and security information: IP address, approximate network location, browser and device attributes, page requests, timestamps and security signals may be processed by hosting, form-delivery and anti-abuse providers.
  • Browser storage: the site uses local storage to remember dismissal of its site notice. Google reCAPTCHA and other third-party tools may set or read their own cookies or comparable identifiers.
  • Correspondence: replies and related email records may be retained by the email service used for communication.

Do not submit passwords, API secrets, authentication tokens, financial credentials, medical records or another person's confidential data through the general contact form.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to receive and respond to enquiries, maintain correspondence, protect the website and its forms against abuse, troubleshoot technical errors, operate the site and meet applicable legal obligations. Contact-form submissions are not intended to subscribe visitors to a newsletter or unrelated promotional messages.

Where EU or UK data protection law applies, the relevant legal bases may include legitimate interests in providing and securing the website, taking steps requested before entering into a contract, compliance with legal obligations, and consent where a particular optional activity legally requires it. The applicable basis depends on the context and activity, not merely on visiting the website.

4. Service providers and third parties

The website uses GitHub Pages for hosting, Formspree for contact submissions, Google reCAPTCHA for anti-spam checks, and Google Fonts to load IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono. Cloudflare is configured for DNS and privacy-focused Web Analytics; the analytics beacon begins collecting aggregate page-view and performance measurements only when enabled on the live website. A separate email provider handles correspondence. Those providers may process information under their own terms, privacy notices and security arrangements. External links may lead to independent websites not controlled by this site.

For details, review GitHub's Privacy Statement, Formspree's Privacy Policy, Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Terms.

5. CAPTCHA, cookies and local storage

The contact experience may use Google's reCAPTCHA service to distinguish legitimate users from automated submissions. Google may process relevant interaction, device and browser signals and may use security-related cookies. For example, Google documents a necessary reCAPTCHA cookie used for risk analysis.

For typography, this website requests IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono from Google Fonts. When the fonts are loaded, the browser connects to Google's font-serving domains, which may receive network details including an IP address and request headers. This service is distinct from Google's reCAPTCHA security checks.

Separately, the site's notice remembers dismissal through the browser preference cookies_accepted=true. The current notice is informational: clicking “Dismiss” only closes the notice and does not selectively activate or block third-party services. It must not be interpreted as a complete advertising or analytics consent-management system.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is a cookie-free analytics service intended to report website traffic, popular pages, approximate geographic trends and performance rather than identify individual readers. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not provide visitors' IP addresses or real-world identities to the site owner. Separately, this website uses a Cloudflare Worker and a private Cloudflare D1 database to record the IP address, approximate country, requested page path and UTC visit time for site-security monitoring, abuse investigation and protection of original content. No name, email address, browser fingerprint or persistent visitor ID is collected by that logging script. These event logs are available only through the site owner's authenticated Cloudflare account and are scheduled for deletion after 30 days. IP addresses can be shared by many people or masked by VPNs and do not reliably identify an individual. Tracking may be blocked or limited by browser privacy features. Visitors may request information or deletion using the contact address above. If other non-essential analytics, marketing pixels or advertising technology are introduced, additional disclosures and, where legally required, prior consent controls will be implemented before those tools operate.

6. Sharing and international processing

Data may be shared with service providers solely as needed to host the site, deliver contact requests, secure services or comply with the law. Information may be stored or processed outside your country, including where a provider's infrastructure operates. When cross-border transfer safeguards are legally required, the relevant processing and provider arrangements must be assessed and implemented before use.

Contact details submitted to this site are not offered for sale as a mailing list. If the business model or data-sharing practices change, this notice must be updated and applicable choice mechanisms added before such processing begins.

7. Retention and deletion

Enquiries and related correspondence are kept for the time reasonably needed to answer the request, manage follow-up conversations, document an agreed engagement or meet legitimate recordkeeping, dispute-resolution and legal requirements. Retention also depends on Formspree and email account settings. Information no longer needed for those purposes should be deleted or anonymised, subject to lawful exceptions.

Private website access-event logs (IP, country, page and time) are scheduled to be deleted after 30 days using a Cloudflare Worker cleanup job. Other enquiries and email correspondence do not have an automatic fixed-day deletion schedule. A documented retention period and operational deletion workflow should be confirmed before the site is used for customer or business records.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and the law that applies, you may be entitled to request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction or objection to particular processing, or withdrawal of consent where consent is the lawful basis. You may also have a right to complain to a competent data protection authority. Rights under the EU GDPR, UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework, or applicable US state privacy laws vary and may be subject to exemptions.

To make a request, email contact@nawazsharif.in with a description of the request. Limited verification may be required to protect your information. The site will respond as applicable law requires; sending a request does not guarantee that all records can lawfully be erased.

9. Security and automated decision-making

Reasonable precautions should be used to protect the website and contact workflow, including access controls, anti-spam measures and provider-managed security. No website, transmission or storage service can guarantee absolute security. Please avoid sending sensitive material through the open enquiry form.

reCAPTCHA may use automated risk analysis to assess possible bot traffic. The website is not intended to make legal, employment, credit or similarly significant decisions about visitors solely by automated processing.

10. Children's information

This site is directed toward a general professional and technical audience and is not designed to solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child submitted personal information, contact the address above so the concern can be reviewed under applicable law.

11. Updates to this notice

New business services, analytics, hosting changes, accounts, payments, monitoring tools or other features may require a revised notice and additional privacy controls. Material updates will be reflected on this page with a new update date. Continuing to use the site does not, by itself, replace consent where affirmative consent is legally required.
