Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Last updated: October 2026

  What core services do you provide?   

I specialize in end-to-end Linux systems engineering, production Kubernetes architecture, zero-trust cybersecurity frameworks, AI/ML infrastructure orchestration with Python, and distributed network deployment.

  What types of projects do you take on?   

I consult and engineer solutions for enterprise cloud migrations, mission-critical server hardening (CIS Benchmarks), DevSecOps CI/CD automation, distributed GPU compute clusters, and high-availability enterprise infrastructure.

  How do you approach infrastructure security and compliance?   

Security is implemented directly at the Linux kernel and container orchestration levels using strict principle-of-least-privilege, Seccomp profiles, cgroups v2, eBPF telemetry, and continuous automated compliance testing against CIS Benchmarks.

  What is your typical engagement model and response timeline?   

Engagements are available on a dedicated technical advisory or fixed-scope project audit basis. Direct inquiries submitted through the contact form are reviewed and acknowledged within 24 business hours.

  Do you offer on-demand troubleshooting for production outages?   

Yes, emergency incident triage is supported for critical server degradation, memory exhaustion, network edge latency issues, and post-exploit containment audits.

  How do you handle remote consultations and global clients?   

Consultations are conducted asynchronously via secure ticketing/email correspondence and synchronously through structured video technical reviews across all major timezones.

  What infrastructure stacks do you regularly operate with?   

Core platforms include Debian/Ubuntu Server, RHEL, Docker, Kubernetes, Ansible, Terraform, NGINX, HAProxy, WireGuard, and major enterprise clouds (AWS, GCP).

  How can we initiate a technical consultation?   

You can submit your initial project scope directly through the Contact page form or email contact@nawazsharif.in with your engagement requirements.
