Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

      

Last updated: October 2026

    
         
      What core services do you provide?       
        I specialize in end-to-end Linux systems engineering, production Kubernetes architecture, zero-trust cybersecurity frameworks, AI/ML infrastructure orchestration with Python, and distributed network deployment.       
    
         
      What types of projects do you take on?       
        I consult and engineer solutions for enterprise cloud migrations, mission-critical server hardening (CIS Benchmarks), DevSecOps CI/CD automation, distributed GPU compute clusters, and high-availability enterprise infrastructure.       
    
         
      How do you approach infrastructure security and compliance?       
        Security is implemented at the kernel and orchestration layers using strict principle-of-least-privilege (PoLP), Seccomp profiles, cgroups v2 resource boundaries, eBPF-based monitoring, and continuous vulnerability scanning aligned with international compliance baselines.       
    
         
      What is your typical engagement model and response timeline?       
        Engagements are available on a dedicated technical advisory or fixed-scope project audit basis. Direct inquiries submitted through the contact channel are reviewed and acknowledged within 24 business hours.
